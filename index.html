Monitored Machines

Air Handling Unit Description

Centralized mechanical system designed to condition and circulate air throughout a facility as part of the overall HVAC system. It regulates indoor air quality, temperature, and humidity by taking in ambient and recirculated air, processing it through multiple internal stages, and distributing it via ductwork.

Chiller

Refrigeration system designed to remove heat from a liquid (typically water or a water-glycol mixture) via a vapor-compression or absorption refrigeration cycle.

Air Compressor

An industrial air compressor converts electrical or mechanical power into potential energy stored in pressurized air. It draws in ambient air, mechanically reduces its volume to increase pressure, and discharges it into a centralized distribution network to power pneumatic machinery, automation systems, and plant instrumentation.

Industrial Boiler

An industrial boiler is a heavy-duty closed pressure vessel engineered to generate steam or high-temperature hot water by burning fuel (such as natural gas, biomass, or fuel oil) or utilizing electric heating elements.

LV Switchgear/Power Distribution Panel

A Low Voltage (LV) Switchgear is a centralized electrical distribution assembly designed to control, protect, and isolate electrical equipment across industrial facilities (operating typically up to 1,000 V AC).

Fan

An industrial fan (or blower) is a dedicated mechanical device designed to generate continuous, high-volume airflow or static pressure to transport air, process gases, and airborne particulates through ductwork and industrial facilities.

Methodology & Formulas

Key Thermodynamic & Efficiency Formulations

Core calculation models running on streaming sensor telemetry to compute plant efficiency metrics:

  • Thermal Efficiency: Evaluates actual useful thermal output versus chemical/electrical input power.
  • Specific Energy Consumption (SEC): Evaluates energy input normalized per unit volume or mass flow.
  • Coefficient of Performance (COP): Evaluates chiller cooling capacity relative to electrical compressor draw.

Data Architecture & APIs

Industrial Ingestion Pipelines

Integration mapping between field instrumentation, industrial historians, and the analytical front-end:

  • AVEVA PI System: Streaming operational time-series data via Web API REST endpoints.
  • TrendMiner: Industrial pattern recognition and event-frame detection integration.
  • Edge Protocols: OPC UA & MQTT brokers standardizing field signals into unified telemetry streams.
